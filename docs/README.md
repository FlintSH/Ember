# Ember documentation

Ember is the Android companion for [Flare](https://github.com/FlintSH/Flare). These guides describe the behavior that ships in this repository's `main` branch. Each release tag holds the documentation for that version.

## Use Ember

- [Install and connect](getting-started.md): requirements, tokens and scopes, local servers, HTTP, and certificates.
- [Share, paste, and shorten](sharing.md): the share menu, Paste to Flare, short links, Quick Settings, and launcher shortcuts.
- [Folder sync](sync.md): albums and folders, options, when sync runs, and which files upload.
- [Library and links](library.md): upload history, your Flare library, and short links.
- [Settings](settings.md): every option and its default.
- [Security and privacy](security.md): what Ember stores, sends, and asks for.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): fixes for connection, upload, and sync problems.

## Work on Ember

- [Architecture](architecture.md): modules, uploads, sync, and the design system.
- [Testing](testing.md): unit, screenshot, live Flare, and emulator tests.
- [Releasing](releasing.md): channels, versioning, CI publishing, and signing.
- [Contributing](../CONTRIBUTING.md)

## Flare's documentation

Ember uses Flare's [API](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/api/) with your own token. Flare's handbook explains [tokens and scopes](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/api/authentication.html), [upload profiles](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/guide/upload-profiles.html), [folders](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/guide/folders.html), [pastes](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/guide/pastes.html), and [short links](https://flintsh.github.io/Flare/guide/short-links.html).
